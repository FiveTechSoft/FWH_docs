CLAUDE.md Example

# FiveWin for Harbour/xHarbour (FWH) - Build Rules
## Building samples
Always use build_new.bat:
  C:\fwteam\samples\build_new.bat <progname> <variant>
Variants: hb32 hm32 hm64 hg32 hg64 hb64 xb32 xb64 xm64

## Critical rules
- strings.prg MUST be CP1252 encoded
- MinGW builds MUST use -static flag
- Dialog templates use CP_ACP not CP_OEMCP
